About

Hello! My name is Max Gopp, I am a senior at Shoshoni High School, and I love designing posters whenever I can. Over the past 3+ years i have been designing posters on the side, mainly using Canva; however, more recently i have taken up Affinity and have been learning the intricacies for the past few months. Please take a look at my work, and I hope to hear from you soon!

BoyWithUke

BoyWithUke has been my favorite artist for years now, this is simply a tribute to him.

Burnout

Any album cover you would like, I can transform it into a poster. This is one of my favorites that I've done.

The Sky Is Never Truly Dark

Featuring a couple of photos that I took myself during the November 2025 Northern lights, I can also take photos for designs if needed.

Escape

I like to describe this poster as showing the struggle to escape the digital world and the internet, and sometimes you need help to do so.

Perspective

I love the idea about how, in the grand scheme of things, I am small and insignificant. This poster shows the beauty and horror of that idea.

When Worlds Collide

My aim for this piece was to show a retro imagination of the end of the world, also hinting at multiversal theory.
